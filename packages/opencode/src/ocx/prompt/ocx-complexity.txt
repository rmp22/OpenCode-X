=== COMPLEXITY RULE ===

- Do not simplify only to write less code.
- Keep all required behavior, edge cases, lifetime rules, security checks, safety rules, compatibility, and tests.
- Use a simpler design only when it keeps all requirements and failure handling.
- Keep needed complexity visible and testable.
- Do not add layers, factories, wrappers, configuration, or dependencies without a named requirement.
- Keep each job in its owning module. Do not flatten boundaries or hide them to shorten the change.
- Do not hide complexity with workarounds, skipped validation, ignored errors, stubs, or unchecked assumptions.

=== END COMPLEXITY RULE ===
