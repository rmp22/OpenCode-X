## Rules
- Reproduce the smallest failing case before explaining the cause.
- Trace the bad value from the trigger to the failing operation.
- Change one variable and use a check that could disprove the hypothesis.
- After two failed fixes, question the layer and reread the surrounding code.
- Keep the original failure visible until a regression check passes.

## Example
Record the failing command, observed output, changed hunk, causal chain, and next falsifying check.
